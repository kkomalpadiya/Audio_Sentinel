Overview

Multi-stage audio risk assessment, run locally

Audio Sentinel prepares an authorized recording, extracts features, detects acoustic events with YAMNet, optionally transcribes speech and checks it against language rules, then combines the evidence into a 0–100 risk score and a consensus decision. Every result is saved as a verifiable local report. An alert is only a candidate for human review; nothing is ever sent.

Implemented stages

Processing pipeline

One evaluation runs these stages in order. Stages 1–4 are interactive in the Feature explorer; the rest run in the backend evaluator.

  1. 1

    Load & validate audio_loader.py

    Accepts WAV or FLAC inside data/raw, checks consent scope and expiry, size, duration, channels and finite samples.

    In explorer
  2. 2

    Prepare pipeline.py · audio_transforms.py

    Resample to 16 kHz, convert to mono, RMS-normalize to −20 dBFS with a −1 dBFS peak ceiling. Spectral-gate noise reduction exists but is off by default.

    In explorer
  3. 3

    Segment segmentation.py

    1 s, 5 s and 10 s windows with 50 % overlap; a short tail is zero-padded and marked.

    In explorer
  4. 4

    Log-Mel features log_mel.py

    Hann window 25 ms, FFT 512, hop 10 ms, 64 Slaney Mel bands to 8 kHz, power in dB (top 80 dB); saved and reloaded with integrity checks.

    In explorer
  5. 5

    Acoustic events acoustic_inference.py · YAMNet v1

    Prepared waveform windows go to YAMNet (521 AudioSet classes), which are mapped to 13 project labels and aggregated above an explicit, uncalibrated threshold.

    Backend
  6. 6

    Speech vad.py · transcription.py

    Only with acoustic + speech consent: Silero VAD finds speech segments; faster-whisper tiny.en transcribes them with reliability checks.

    Backend
  7. 7

    Language rules language_analysis.py

    Distress, threat, weapon-reference and ambiguous categories; negated, quoted or hypothetical phrasing is suppressed. Transcript text is not written to reports or evidence files.

    Backend
  8. 8

    Risk score risk_scoring.py

    Rule set v1: acoustic ≤ 60, speech ≤ 12, language ≤ 50 points, capped at 100 and mapped to none / low / medium / high / critical.

    Backend
  9. 9

    Consensus decision consensus_service.py

    Chooses no action, log, review or alert. An alert needs a critical score and two agreeing branches, and is only a local candidate.

    Backend
  10. 10

    Report & audit final_report.py · alert_audit.py

    Hash-bound final report and immutable local audit record; notification delivery is permanently “not sent”.

    Backend

Missing evidence, unreliable transcripts or ambiguous language force review regardless of score.

What the prototype does not claim

  • It does not confirm emergencies; every alert is a pending candidate for a person to review.
  • No notification is sent and no recipient can be configured.
  • The acoustic threshold and rule weights are experimental and uncalibrated.
  • The final deployment review recorded ten release blockers; the decision is not_approved_for_production.
  • Use is limited to authorized, consented recordings in a controlled local setting.
